13 Check ingredients — after import

Follows "Read recipe". Name matches against household products are linked automatically. One card is open at a time. The unit switch converts the stored amount: cups use ¼ ½ ¾ glyphs (BLOC's), and when converting into cups, the result snaps to the nearest quarter and shows "≈". Conversions only go to or from cups: ml ↔ cup is fixed arithmetic, g ↔ cup needs the ingredient's density (disabled, with the reason, when it's unknown), and g ↔ ml is never offered. So the switch shows the ingredient's own unit plus cup; an amount in cups can go to either g or ml.
