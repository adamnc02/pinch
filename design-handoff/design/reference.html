04 Recipe sheet — Method (after flip)

Pinch
Dinner · Italy

Spaghetti carbonara

Serves
recipe serves 4
6
  1. 1

    Cut the guanciale into strips and render slowly in a cold pan until crisp.

    Guanciale 225 g

  2. 2

    Whisk the yolks and eggs with most of the cheese and plenty of pepper.

    Egg yolks 6 · Whole eggs 3 · Pecorino Romano 90 g · Black pepper 1 tbsp

  3. 3

    Boil the spaghetti in well-salted water until just shy of al dente.

    Spaghetti 600 g

  4. 4

    Off the heat, toss with the guanciale, then the egg mix, loosening with pasta water. Finish with Parmigiano.

    Parmigiano Reggiano 45 g
